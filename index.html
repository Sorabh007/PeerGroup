How a session works
01

You post your revision topic

Set your subject and a time. Your name, gender, age — none of it shows. Only your academic credentials are visible.

02

Only the most qualified peer joins

Applicants take an AI-generated quiz on your topic. The top scorer gets the seat — no favourites, no randoms.

03

You lead. They sharpen.

You drive the session at your pace. Your peer's one job: catch what you glossed over. You both leave more complete than when you arrived. That's the barter.
